# Chromium third-party notices

This launcher redistributes an unmodified official Chromium snapshot. The package includes the Chromium `LICENSE` pinned by `chromium.lock.json`. Chromium's embedded `chrome://credits` page contains the notices and license text for third-party components included in the binary.

Source revision, archive URL, SHA-256, Chromium commit, license URL, and license SHA-256 are recorded in `chromium.lock.json` and `BUILD-METADATA.json`. CGC-PMS does not claim endorsement by Chromium or its contributors.
